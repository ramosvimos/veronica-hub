Resident Evil Veronica follows Claire Redfield after the Raccoon City disaster as she searches for Chris Redfield and is captured by Umbrella forces. Official store context points to Rockfort Island and the survival-horror premise, but Veronica Hub avoids treating every detail from the 2000 game as automatically confirmed for the remake.

That separation matters. A remake can preserve major story beats while changing scenes, pacing, enemy placement, voice direction or gameplay structure. The story page can mention original-game context when it helps readers understand the premise, but it must label that context clearly and keep remake-confirmed claims tied to official sources.

The goal is to help new players understand why the game matters without spoiling the full original plot. As new official trailers, screenshots or store descriptions appear, this page should update the confirmed context and move older assumptions into the original-versus-remake comparison page.
