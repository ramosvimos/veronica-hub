Resident Evil Veronica source-backed official update tracker

Resident Evil Veronica Story

A spoiler-light overview of Claire Redfield, Rockfort Island and the official remake premise.

Last checked: 2026-07-19. Exact release date has not been announced.

Steam places the story three months after the biological disaster in Raccoon City. Claire Redfield travels to France in search of Chris Redfield after surviving with Leon S. Kennedy and Sherry Birkin.

The official store synopsis says Claire is captured by Umbrella special forces and taken to Rockfort Island, which is transformed by another biological disaster into a survival-horror setting.

A producer interview frames the remake as a chance to continue Claire's post-Raccoon City story and explore more of her character motivation, but exact scene changes remain undisclosed.

The original Code: Veronica remains useful context, but the remake is officially described as a reimagined storyline. This page avoids treating every original scene, puzzle, enemy or character beat as remake-confirmed.

For a spoiler-light reading, the confirmed setup has three anchors: Claire's search for Chris, Umbrella's capture of Claire, and the Rockfort Island outbreak. Those anchors are enough to understand why the remake matters without listing late-game twists from the original.

The remake label creates an important boundary. It confirms a relationship to Code: Veronica, but it does not promise that every cutscene, boss order, puzzle solution or supporting character will survive unchanged.

This page therefore separates premise from prediction. Premise comes from current official copy. Prediction belongs in comparison notes and should be phrased as context, not confirmation.

Readers who have not played the original can use this page without learning every reveal. Readers who did play the original can use Original vs Remake to compare direction while waiting for Capcom to show more story material.
