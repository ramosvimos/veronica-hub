Frequently Asked Questions

Is Resident Evil Code Veronica Remake official?

Yes. Capcom announced Resident Evil Veronica, a remake of Resident Evil Code: Veronica, with a planned 2027 release window.

What is the exact release date?

A 2027 release window is confirmed. An exact release date has not been officially confirmed.

What platforms are confirmed?

Official sources list PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2 and PC. Steam also has a live store page.

Are PC system requirements confirmed?

No. PC is confirmed, but minimum and recommended PC requirements are not officially confirmed yet. Veronica Hub shows a separate speculative estimate based on official Steam requirements from nearby RE Engine games.

Can I use the estimated PC requirements to buy hardware?

Treat the estimate as preparation guidance, not an official purchase guarantee. Wait for Capcom or Steam to publish Resident Evil Veronica requirements before making a final upgrade decision.

Is there a demo?

No official demo has been confirmed at the current verification time.

Are preorders or editions available?

Wishlist access exists on Steam, but preorder timing, price and editions are not officially confirmed yet.

Is Veronica Hub an official Capcom site?

No. Veronica Hub is an independent fan-made information site and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Capcom.
