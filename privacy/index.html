Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Veronica Hub collects, uses and links to when you visit the site.

Last checked: 2026-07-26. Independent fan-made site policy page.

Veronica Hub is an independent fan-made information site. The site does not ask visitors to create accounts, submit passwords or provide payment information.

The site may use basic analytics to understand page traffic, source-link clicks, trailer plays, language switches, device categories, referrers and performance issues. Analytics data is used to improve navigation, page quality and technical reliability.

The site is configured for Google AdSense. Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this and other websites. Google's advertising cookies enable Google and its partners to serve personalized or non-personalized ads where ads are shown.

Visitors can learn about Google advertising controls and opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ads Settings. Visitors can also use browser settings or regional privacy tools to limit cookies where available.

Third-party links on this site may lead to Capcom, Steam, YouTube, platform-holder stores, media sites or other external websites. Those websites operate under their own privacy policies and terms.
