Resident Evil Veronica Release Date

The official release window is 2027. No exact release date is confirmed yet.

Last verified: 2026-06-10. Exact release date: not officially confirmed unless a source record says otherwise.

The current verified release information is narrow but important. Resident Evil Veronica has an official 2027 release window, and that window is supported by Capcom announcement material and the Steam store page. Veronica Hub does not publish specific calendar dates unless they appear in official material or a high-reliability platform listing.

That means dates circulating through forum posts, social clips, shop placeholders, countdown sites or unsourced images should not be treated as confirmed. They may be useful context for fans, but they are not enough to change the release date claim on this page. The public claim remains 2027 until Capcom or an official store updates the wording.

This page also tracks what should change when more information arrives. The exact date, preorder date, demo timing and edition availability are separate claims. Keeping them separate prevents a single vague announcement from being stretched into details that have not been confirmed.
