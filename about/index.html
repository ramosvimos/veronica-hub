About Veronica Hub

Veronica Hub is an independent fan-made source tracker for Resident Evil Veronica.

Last checked: 2026-07-26. Independent fan-made site policy page.

Veronica Hub exists to help readers follow Resident Evil Veronica without mixing confirmed information, reported context and rumor. The site is independently operated and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Capcom.

The editorial rule is simple: official facts come from Capcom, official platform stores or verified official video channels. Reported interview details are labeled as reported context. Rumors, retailer placeholders, unsourced screenshots and social reposts are not treated as confirmed information.

The site brings release, platform, PC, trailer and story sources together so readers can check a claim without hunting through several store pages.

Resident Evil, Resident Evil Veronica, Code: Veronica, Capcom and related names are trademarks or property of their respective owners. This site uses those names only to identify the game and discuss publicly available information.
