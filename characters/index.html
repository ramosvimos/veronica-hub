The characters page tracks names that appear in official Resident Evil Veronica material and separates them from original-game context. Claire Redfield is central to the premise, Chris Redfield is part of the search that drives the story context, and other characters should be listed carefully according to what official materials confirm.

This page should not become a full wiki copied from the original game. The more useful structure is status-based: confirmed in remake material, referenced by official context, known from the original game, or not yet confirmed for the remake. That gives fans useful orientation without pretending every original detail is already locked.

Official screenshots and trailer frames can support character presentation, but they should not be used to overclaim. If a character appears visually but is not named in current material, the entry should say what is visible and what remains unconfirmed.
