## Revisions and claim controls

- Product scope is restricted to documented public Instagram speech transcription. No OCR, arbitrary social-platform, API, automatic newsletter-generation, or sending capability is implied.
- Pricing and volatile allowance figures are omitted. Troubleshooting directs readers to their current account screen and official pricing information.
- New examples and checklists are original editorial guidance. No borrowed article structure, invented customer story, fabricated result, ranking, traffic volume, or keyword-volume claim is used.
- Publication permission is treated as a separate question. Public availability is not presented as blanket reuse authorization; no legal conclusion is given for any particular quotation.
- Humanization review removed generic openings and promotional claims. Each section begins with a useful decision or action; sentences vary in length. Repeated cautions were kept only where they prevent a distinct practical error.
- Metadata was revised after the editorial pass; the automatic-caption excerpt was brought to 155 characters. All source URLs and certainty levels were rechecked after revision.
- No images were generated or placeholder image references inserted; this delivery is the requested text-guide set.

## Remaining limits

Public documentation can change after the review date. Authenticated interface labels, actual processing performance, download behavior, third-party editor support, and service availability were not independently exercised. These guides are practical editorial workflows, not independent performance reviews or product endorsements.
