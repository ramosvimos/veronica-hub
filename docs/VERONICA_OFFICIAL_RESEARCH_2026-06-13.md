## Claims Added Or Strengthened

- `remake-status`: confirmed remake of 2000 Resident Evil Code: Veronica.
- `modernized-gameplay`: Steam/Capcom wording around modernized gameplay, reimagined story and detailed graphics.
- `story-premise`: Claire, Chris search motivation, Umbrella special forces and Rockfort Island premise.
- `steam-features`: Steam feature flags are store-reported, not gameplay guarantees beyond the store listing.
- `supported-languages`: Steam language listing is store-reported.

## Claims Still Unknown

- Exact release date.
- Preorder timing and price.
- Demo availability.
- Editions and bonus content.
- Final PC requirements.
- Whether every original Code: Veronica character, scene, enemy, puzzle or gameplay beat appears in the remake.

## Editorial Rules

- Capcom and platform/store pages are primary sources.
- Media interviews can be cited only in a separate interview/press section and should not silently upgrade a claim to confirmed.
- Reddit, forums, leaks and retailer placeholders are not source material for confirmed facts.
- Original-game details are background context, not remake-confirmed information.
