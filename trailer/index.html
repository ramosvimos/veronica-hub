Resident Evil Veronica Trailer

Watch the English announcement trailer and find the verified Capcom and platform-channel uploads.

Last checked: 2026-08-03. Exact release date has not been announced.

The main video is the English announcement trailer from the official Resident Evil YouTube channel. It loads after you click the poster.

Trailer footage can show tone, setting and characters, but it does not confirm every mechanic, enemy name, puzzle structure or chapter sequence. Media Q&A reports say producer Yoshiaki Hirabayashi described the remake as a third-person game, so the first-person-looking trailer section should not be treated as a confirmed first-person mode.

The official video library below includes Capcom regional uploads, platform-holder uploads and the Summer Game Fest world-premiere upload. Media-site mirrors are not treated as primary official records.

The trailer does not confirm a demo, exact date, editions, final PC requirements, a full enemy list or every returning character.

Official uploads can carry different descriptions, timestamps and regional details, which is why the page keeps Capcom, platform-holder and event versions separate. A repost may preserve the footage but lose the source context needed to confirm a claim.

For gameplay questions, a developer presentation or store feature listing is stronger evidence than a cinematic camera angle. Until Capcom shows an extended gameplay sequence, the trailer should be read as a reveal of premise and tone.
