Resident Evil Veronica Platforms

Confirmed platforms are PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2 and PC.

Last checked: 2026-08-03. Exact release date has not been announced.

Resident Evil Veronica is confirmed for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2 and PC. Official store pages are live on Steam, PlayStation, Xbox and Nintendo, with direct links provided below.

No older-console, subscription or cloud release has been announced.

The official Xbox listing currently shows single-player, Xbox achievements, cloud saves, 4K Ultra HD and Series X|S optimization. These store flags do not confirm a 4K 60 FPS performance target.

The Xbox page displays a Mature 17+ badge and content descriptors, but the same page also says the game has not yet been rated. The final ESRB rating is therefore still unclear.

The PlayStation listing currently shows offline single-player, DualSense vibration and trigger support, PS5 Pro enhancement and Game Help. The Nintendo listing confirms a digital Switch 2 version, one local player and TV, tabletop and handheld play modes.

Capcom also reported more than one million combined PS5 and PC wishlists shortly after the announcement. Wishlist interest is not the same as sales, preorders or a release-date commitment.

Physical editions, cross-save, cloud streaming, performance modes, regional timing and subscription availability remain unannounced.

Steam confirms a PC release and wishlist access. It does not yet list final hardware requirements, price, DRM details, preorder timing or Steam Deck support.

Capcom has not published resolution, frame-rate or packaging details for the Nintendo Switch 2 version.
