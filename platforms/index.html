Resident Evil Veronica source-backed official update tracker

Resident Evil Veronica Platforms

Confirmed platforms are PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2 and PC.

Last checked: 2026-07-19. Exact release date has not been announced.

Resident Evil Veronica is confirmed for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2 and PC. Official store pages are live on Steam, PlayStation, Xbox and Nintendo, with direct links provided below.

Other formats, subscription launches, cloud versions or older-console releases have not been confirmed. They should not be assumed from previous Resident Evil releases.

The official Xbox listing currently shows single-player, Xbox achievements, cloud saves, 4K Ultra HD and Series X|S optimization. These store flags do not confirm a 4K 60 FPS performance target.

The PlayStation listing currently shows offline single-player, DualSense vibration and trigger support, PS5 Pro enhancement and Game Help. The Nintendo listing confirms a digital Switch 2 version, one local player and TV, tabletop and handheld play modes.

Capcom also reported more than one million combined PS5 and PC wishlists shortly after the announcement. Wishlist interest is not the same as sales, preorders or a release-date commitment.

The confirmed platform list is already useful for purchase planning, but it does not answer every platform question. Physical editions, cross-save, cloud streaming, performance modes, regional storefront timing and subscription availability are separate details that still need official confirmation.

PC has the clearest store signal because the Steam page is live. That confirms PC presence and wishlist access, but it does not confirm the final hardware requirements, pricing, DRM details, preorder timing or Steam Deck status.

Nintendo Switch 2 support is notable because it means the remake is being announced for current-generation Nintendo hardware from the start. Until Capcom publishes more technical information, this page should avoid guessing resolution, frame rate or cartridge/download packaging.

The page intentionally keeps unsupported platforms out of the confirmed list. Prior Resident Evil releases can suggest patterns, but platform support for this remake should come from the current Resident Evil Veronica announcement cycle, not from assumptions about earlier games.
