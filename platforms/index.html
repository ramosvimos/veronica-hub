Resident Evil Veronica is currently tracked for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2 and PC. PC status is also supported by the live Steam page. This page keeps platform confirmation separate from store features, because a platform can be confirmed before every store-specific detail is available.

The platform list should not be expanded from expectation alone. Older platforms, cloud versions, subscription availability and collector editions need their own sources before they appear as confirmed. If a store page or platform holder adds a new listing, the source record and affected claim should be updated together.

Platform pages are useful because players often search by the device they own. Veronica Hub should answer those searches directly while keeping uncertainty visible. If a platform has no confirmed release, the page should say unknown rather than hide the gap.
