Resident Evil Veronica Sources

Official links used for release window, platforms, trailer, store status and media.

Last checked: 2026-07-19. Exact release date has not been announced.

This page collects the official links used across the site, including Capcom announcement material, the official Resident Evil Veronica product page, the Steam store page and verified official video uploads.

Capcom sources confirm the announcement, release window, platform set, remake positioning and RE Engine development context. Steam confirms store-specific details including developer, publisher, planned release year, story synopsis, feature flags, language support and TBD PC requirements. PlayStation, Xbox and Nintendo records add current platform-specific store capabilities.

Media interviews and Q&A reports are now tracked separately as reported context, useful for producer comments about camera direction, naming and development-team background. Leaks, screenshots without links and retailer placeholders are not treated as confirmed information unless they are backed by an official source.

The source hierarchy is intentionally conservative. Official Capcom pages, official store pages and verified platform-holder uploads are the strongest records. Interviews and event Q&A coverage can add context, but they are labeled as reported unless Capcom publishes the same detail in first-party copy.

The page also records what each source is used for. A trailer upload might confirm video availability and tone, while the Steam page is better for PC store status, language listings and system requirement placeholders.

This prevents one source from carrying more weight than it should. For example, a store page can confirm wishlist access, but it does not confirm preorder availability unless the store actually opens preorders.

If a source changes, the affected claims should be updated with the new check date. If a source disappears or becomes ambiguous, the claim should be downgraded until a stable official replacement is available.

Developer & Publisher

CAPCOM Co., Ltd.

Developer and publisher are the same company

Resident Evil Veronica lists CAPCOM Co., Ltd. as both developer and publisher on Steam. In practical terms, the developer role covers making the game, including design, engineering, art, audio and production. The publisher role covers commercial release work such as platform coordination, store pages, marketing, localization, age ratings and launch operations. Because Capcom is both developer and publisher here, official Capcom and store channels are especially important sources for factual updates.

Reference links: Steam store, Capcom product index, Capcom Platinum Titles.
