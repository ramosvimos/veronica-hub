Resident Evil Veronica Sources

Official links used for release window, platforms, trailer, store status and media.

Last checked: 2026-07-26. Exact release date has not been announced.

Capcom announcements and product pages are the main sources for the remake's release window, platforms and overall direction.

Steam, PlayStation, Xbox and Nintendo store pages provide platform-specific details such as wishlist access, supported languages and feature labels.

Attributed interviews add producer comments about the camera, title, development team and story. They are labeled as reported until Capcom publishes the same detail directly.

Retailer placeholders, screenshots without a source and social reposts are not used as confirmation.

The Xbox listing currently contradicts itself: it shows a Mature 17+ badge while also saying the game has not yet been rated. The rating remains unresolved.

The source hierarchy is deliberate: first-party Capcom records and official platform stores carry the most weight, verified video uploads confirm what was published, and attributed interviews supply context with a reported label. A source is only used for the claim it actually supports.

Developer & Publisher

CAPCOM Co., Ltd.

Developer and publisher are the same company

Resident Evil Veronica lists CAPCOM Co., Ltd. as both developer and publisher on Steam. In practical terms, the developer role covers making the game, including design, engineering, art, audio and production. The publisher role covers commercial release work such as platform coordination, store pages, marketing, localization, age ratings and launch operations. Because Capcom is both developer and publisher here, official Capcom and store channels are especially important sources for factual updates.

Reference links: Steam store, Capcom product index, Capcom Platinum Titles.
