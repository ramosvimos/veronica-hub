Original vs Remake

A careful comparison between official remake material and original-game context.

Last checked: 2026-06-13. Exact release date has not been announced.

Resident Evil Veronica is officially described as a remake of 2000's Resident Evil Code: Veronica. Steam says it preserves the essence of the original while adding modernized gameplay, a reimagined storyline and detailed graphics.

The original game is useful context for Claire Redfield, Rockfort Island, Umbrella and the wider Resident Evil timeline, but remake status should come from current official material.

This comparison is meant to help returning fans and new players understand what is officially stated now, what comes from the original game, and what remains unknown.
