Original vs Remake

A careful comparison between official remake material and original-game context.

Last verified: 2026-06-10. Exact release date: not officially confirmed unless a source record says otherwise.

Resident Evil Veronica is a remake of Resident Evil Code: Veronica, but a remake is not a line-by-line confirmation of every original-game detail. This page compares what is officially known about the remake with the original game's context and keeps the labels visible.

The original game can help explain why fans care about Claire Redfield, Rockfort Island, Umbrella and the broader Resident Evil timeline. It should not be used as proof of remake mechanics, enemy behavior, exact scene order, puzzle structure or final content unless official material confirms those details.

This distinction lets Veronica Hub serve both returning fans and new players. Returning fans get context without misinformation, while new players can understand the premise without reading a full spoiler wiki.
