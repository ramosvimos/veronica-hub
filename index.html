Independent tracker for official Resident Evil Veronica updates

Resident Evil Veronica Tracker

Resident Evil Veronica is Capcom's official remake of the 2000 classic, confirmed for a 2027 release window on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Switch 2 and PC.

Last checked: 2026-09-08. Exact release date has not been announced.

Capcom officially describes the project as a reimagining of Resident Evil Code: Veronica, updating the original foundation with modernized gameplay mechanics and more detailed graphics. The remake has a confirmed 2027 release window across PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2 and PC via Steam.

Official store links point readers to the Steam, PlayStation, Xbox and Nintendo listings; availability and regional visibility can vary by platform.

On August 4, 2026, the official Resident Evil account reported that Resident Evil Veronica had reached two million wishlists. This is an interest milestone, not a sales figure or preorder count.

Developer interviews report a modern third-person camera direction and involvement from members of the Resident Evil 2 and Resident Evil 4 remake development teams. PlayStation Blog coverage also notes that elements of the classic narrative will be adjusted to better align with the broader series lore established since 2000.

Technical specifications remain unconfirmed. While the Steam product page is live, minimum and recommended PC hardware requirements are listed as to be determined, with further platform details pending future Capcom announcements.

September 8 review: Capcom's September 1 announcement schedules a roughly 40-minute Spotlight for September 16 at 14:00 UTC. Veronica is not named in that program list; an appearance or reveal remains unconfirmed.
