Resident Evil Veronica Release Date

Capcom has officially confirmed a 2027 release window for Resident Evil Veronica, but an exact launch date has not been announced.

Last checked: 2026-09-08. Exact release date has not been announced.

Capcom and official store listings place Resident Evil Veronica in a 2027 release window. None currently lists a season, month or day.

An exact release date, preorder date and launch price have not been announced.

Retailer placeholders, countdown pages and social posts are not release-date confirmation. Treat a date as official only when it appears in Capcom material or a matching official store update.

Wishlisting is available on several stores, but a wishlist button does not mean preorders are open.

A release window is not a launch date. The confirmed information currently stops at the year, so quarter, month and day claims need a dated Capcom announcement or matching official-store update.

Treat announcement, exact-date reveal, preorder opening and launch as separate events. The announcement and store wishlists are live; the exact date and preorder checkout are not confirmed.

Reviewed September 8, 2026: the checked Capcom and platform-store records still give only 2027. A publisher showcase date is not a game release date.
