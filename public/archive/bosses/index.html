Code Veronica Bosses: Spoiler-Controlled Original Dossiers

Read selected original Code: Veronica encounter dossiers with spoiler controls. These are not confirmed Resident Evil Veronica remake bosses.

Last checked: 2026-09-08. Exact release date has not been announced.

By Veronica Hub editorial deskPublished 2026-09-08Last checked 2026-09-08

Original-game material is labeled separately. Archived lore is not a remake appearance announcement.

These dossiers explain selected creatures in Capcom’s original-game archive. They do not claim that the remake keeps the same bosses, forms, encounter order or solutions. No complete remake boss list is established by these older sources.

Names and plot details in the dossiers below can reveal major original-story turns. Each stays closed until you choose to open it. Source pages can contain additional spoilers beyond this guide.

Original dossier 1 — reveal identity and story spoilers
Original-game archive

Nosferatu

Capcom identifies Nosferatu as Alexander Ashford after a failed response to the t-Veronica virus. Its archive describes an exposed heart, abnormal appendages and a toxin that becomes a dangerous mist.

This explains the original creature’s identity and visual language. It does not verify a remake boss, weak point or combat solution.

Sources: Capcom creature archive: Nosferatu

Original dossier 2 — reveal late-story transformations
Original-game archive

Alexia’s transformations

The archive describes Alexia adapting to her own t-Veronica creation through fifteen years of suspended animation. Her original encounters involve multiple transformations, including blood that reacts with air.

The old form sequence should not be treated as a remake design document. This page deliberately avoids presenting the original final-weapon solution as advice for the new game.

Sources: Capcom creature archive: Alexia

Original dossier 3 — reveal a character’s fate
Original-game archive

Steve’s transformed encounter

Capcom’s archive describes Steve’s mutation after Alexia administers t-Veronica, and his brief recovery of control to protect Claire. This is a major original-story event.

It belongs in an encounter dossier, not an assertion that every major creature is a conventional boss fight. Neither this scene nor its resolution is established for the remake by the archive.

Sources: Capcom creature archive: Monster Steve
