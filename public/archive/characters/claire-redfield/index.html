Official remake / announcement

Claire in the remake premise

The remake’s official store synopsis follows Claire’s search for Chris and her capture by Umbrella. Her position at the center of the premise is different from confirmation of a complete playable-character roster.

Sources: Steam store

Original-game archive

From Raccoon City to Rockfort Island

Capcom’s Claire archive presents her as a university student looking for her brother when she becomes caught in Raccoon City’s outbreak. Leon helps her survive; the archive later links him to informing Chris about her captivity on Rockfort Island.

That background explains why Claire, Chris and Leon appear together in discussions of this period. Mentioning Leon as part of continuity does not itself establish an on-screen remake role.

Sources: Capcom digital archive: Claire Redfield

Original-game archive

The red-jacket connection

Capcom’s costume retrospective places Claire’s RE2, Code: Veronica and Revelations 2 outfits side by side and highlights her red biker-jacket identity. It is historical costume context, not a confirmation of unlockable outfits or paid remake costumes.

Sources: Capcom: Dressed to Kill
