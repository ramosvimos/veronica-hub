Place her name in the family

Capcom’s Nosferatu archive identifies Alexia as Alexander Ashford’s daughter and refers to Alfred as her brother. Their family connection is the starting point for the separate Alfred dossier.

Original-game archive

Alexia and t-Veronica

The official creature archive credits Alexia with creating t-Veronica and describes her fifteen-year suspended animation as the means by which she adapted to it. It also records three forms in the original final encounters.

This page leaves the detailed combat sequence to the spoiler-controlled Bosses guide rather than retelling every phase here.

Sources: Capcom creature archive: Alexia

Reading guide

What the archive cannot confirm

A retrospective entry is not evidence of a new model, voice actor, boss design or exact scene. Even when a remake draws on the same story, those are individual production and design choices that need current evidence.

For that reason, this dossier does not label an old image as remake concept art or turn an original encounter description into a current feature list. Its status can change only when suitable new material identifies the character or detail.

Reading guide

Alexia, Alfred and Alexander are not interchangeable

Similar names make it easy to misread a discussion of the Ashfords. Follow the individual character links instead of assuming a shared surname means the same role. Keep the family-background reading separate from gameplay questions and from predictions about a future trailer.

The source links are provided for verification, but the archives themselves may show images and explanations that reveal more than this page’s open sections.
