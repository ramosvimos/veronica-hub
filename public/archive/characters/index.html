Resident Evil Veronica Characters

Claire Redfield is the central focus of the Resident Evil Veronica remake, while the full cast and playable roster remain unannounced.

Last checked: 2026-09-08. Exact release date has not been announced.

According to the official Steam premise, Claire Redfield is the primary focus of the story as she searches for her missing brother. Chris Redfield is named in this core narrative setup, but Capcom has not detailed the extent of his role, screen time or playability in the remake.

During story discussions, producer Yoshiaki Hirabayashi mentioned Albert Wesker alongside Claire and Chris. This narrative context should not be treated as a formal cast announcement or a playable-character reveal.

Individuals known only from the original 2000 release remain part of the classic-game context. Until Capcom formally confirms them in the remake, their return remains unconfirmed.
