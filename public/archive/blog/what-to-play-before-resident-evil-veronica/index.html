What to Play Before Resident Evil Veronica: A Spoiler-Light Guide

Start with Claire, add Chris’s background when useful, or go in fresh. A focused preparation guide without turning the entire series into homework.

Last checked: 2026-09-08. Exact release date has not been announced.

By Veronica Hub editorial deskPublished 2026-09-08Last checked 2026-09-08About 4 min read

← Blog

An independently written feature. Source facts, original-game context and editorial analysis are labeled separately.

Preparing for Resident Evil Veronica can be a short, focused journey rather than a franchise-completion project. Choose the background that answers your questions, then leave room to discover the new game for yourself.

Our recommendation is to choose a preparation route based on what you want: Claire’s immediate background, the Redfield family’s earlier history, or the experience of comparing an old game with its remake. There is no single mandatory marathon.

Attributed producer report

The producer does not require an original playthrough

You do not need to turn Resident Evil Veronica into a franchise-completion project. In Famitsu’s producer Q&A, Hirabayashi says the remake is intended to be enjoyable without playing the original, while familiarity with other Resident Evil stories can add context.

Sources: Famitsu: producer Q&A at SGF (June 11, 2026)

Original-game archive

Only one game? Start with the RE2 remake

The Resident Evil 2 Steam description offers separately playable Leon and Claire campaigns, built around the Raccoon City outbreak. It identifies Claire as a college student searching for her brother and describes exploration, puzzles and an over-the-shoulder camera. This makes Claire’s campaign a direct introduction to the person at the center of Veronica.

Sources: Resident Evil 2 Steam listing

Editorial analysis

Why that is our first recommendation

Starting with Claire gives the next story a personal question rather than a list of unfamiliar names: what does she do after surviving an event that should have ended an ordinary life? You are following the same person instead of trying to memorize every organization in the series.

Play Leon’s perspective as well when the broader picture interests you, but do not treat every extra mode or unlock as preparation you must finish. A first playthrough should establish your interest in these characters. Completion statistics are not an entrance requirement for the next game.

Original-game archive

Add the first Resident Evil for Chris’s background

The HD remaster’s store page places the first game’s mansion incident in 1998. Capcom’s Chris archive identifies him as a S.T.A.R.S. survivor of that incident. Together, those sources give an entry point for understanding why Claire’s brother already has a history with the series’ disasters.

Sources: Resident Evil HD remaster Steam listing · Capcom digital archive: Chris Redfield

Editorial analysis

Pick your second step by curiosity, not obligation

Our longer route is the first Resident Evil, then Claire’s RE2 story, followed by Veronica when it arrives. The attraction is context: you first meet the brother, then the sister looking for him. Our shorter route simply begins with Claire. Both are reading choices, not an official Capcom play-order announcement.

The HD remaster lists an alternative control scheme alongside its original one. That is worth checking when deciding whether you would enjoy the older presentation. It does not tell us which controls Veronica will offer; it only helps you judge this optional preparation game on its own terms.

Sources: Resident Evil HD remaster Steam listing

Editorial analysis

Should you play the original Code: Veronica first?

Play it first to compare interpretations: how a familiar setting, character exchange or threat is reconsidered. Wait to discover the story fresh. Those are different pleasures, and neither is the more authentic way to arrive at a remake.

A full story video has the same basic spoiler tradeoff as playing the old game. Before pressing play, decide whether you want background or the ending. A spoiler-light synopsis can supply the former without giving away the latter. It is a better fit when your main interest is the new game rather than an original-versus-remake comparison.

Official remake / announcement

The minimum context is already enough

Veronica’s current store premise begins three months after Raccoon City. Claire travels to France looking for Chris, is captured by Umbrella forces and is taken to Rockfort Island. That is the setup you need—not a recap of every later twist.

Sources: Steam store

Reading guide

Choose a route and stop when you have enough context

New to the series: begin with Claire in RE2. Curious about Chris: add the first game. Interested in historical comparison: include the original Code: Veronica. Keen to avoid spoilers: read the premise and leave the deeper dossiers closed.

Check the exact edition and supported platform before buying an older game. This feature does not assume that every release is available on every system, quote a temporary sale as a permanent price, or promise that a subscription includes it.
