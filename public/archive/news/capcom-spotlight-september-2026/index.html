Reading guide

A showcase is not a release date

There are three separate questions: when a publisher broadcasts, which games it names, and what each game actually announces. Treating an answer to the first question as an answer to all three is how an event reminder becomes a misleading headline.

For the same reason, an on-site booth activity should not be assumed to be a downloadable demo. A useful demo announcement needs the game’s name, supported platforms and availability details, not just the publisher’s presence at a show.

Reading guide

What would change this report?

A named Veronica segment, an official video upload or a dated Capcom announcement would provide new evidence. A trailer could update the media library without opening preorders; a date announcement could update the release page without confirming a demo. Each development should be recorded separately.

The source below is the starting announcement, not a live countdown or a claim to have watched the future broadcast. Check its current wording before making plans around the event.
