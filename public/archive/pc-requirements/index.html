Resident Evil Veronica PC Requirements

Resident Evil Veronica is confirmed for PC via Steam, but minimum and recommended system requirements are still to be determined.

Last checked: 2026-09-08. Exact release date has not been announced.

Capcom has officially confirmed that Resident Evil Veronica is coming to PC, and the official Steam store listing is active for wishlisting. The hardware fields for both minimum and recommended configurations remain labeled as to be determined.

Hardware requirements from nearby RE Engine releases serve strictly as contextual planning references, not as specifications or performance guarantees for Resident Evil Veronica.

Do not buy or upgrade hardware for this game based on estimates alone. Wait for Capcom or Steam to publish the final CPU, GPU, memory and storage requirements.

Capcom has not confirmed target resolutions, frame rates, CPU models, graphics cards, RAM requirements or installation storage sizes. Ray tracing, upscaling technology and official Steam Deck compatibility also remain unannounced.
