Resident Evil Veronica Steam Release

The official Steam page for Resident Evil Veronica is live with wishlist access, a planned 2027 release year and PC requirements still marked TBD.

Last checked: 2026-09-08. Exact release date has not been announced.

The official Steam page lists CAPCOM as developer and publisher and provides wishlist access for the PC version.

Steam currently shows a planned 2027 release year, while minimum and recommended PC system requirements remain to be determined.

Wishlist access is not preorder availability. Price, editions, preorder bonuses and an exact release date remain unknown until Steam or Capcom publishes those details.

The Steam page lists single-player, Steam Achievements, subtitles, HDR, Family Sharing and support for fourteen languages as store fields.

PC is confirmed, but final requirements, price, editions, preload timing and Steam Deck compatibility remain unconfirmed.
