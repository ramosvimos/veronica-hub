Bandersnatch: the long-reaching prototype

Capcom’s archive describes a human-derived prototype designed for practical deployment. Its distinguishing feature is a rapidly extending right arm; its weakened lower body limits mobility. The arm also helps it pursue targets across different levels.

That asymmetrical silhouette is the useful identification cue in the original design. Whether a remake preserves its proportions, traversal or attack behavior requires new evidence.

Sources: Capcom creature archive: Bandersnatch

Original-game archive

Zombies: an original-series reference

Capcom describes zombies as humans affected by t-Virus infection, with progressive decay and a constant demand for energy. The familiar human outline makes them distinct from the engineered, asymmetrical Bandersnatch.

This archive establishes older creature background, not remake health values or a guarantee that a familiar combat tactic will work unchanged.

Sources: Capcom creature archive: Zombie

Reading guide

How this guide handles an unidentified creature

A source image can establish that a creature was shown without establishing its name. A useful identification record should link the official image or video, describe what can actually be seen, and keep a guessed identity separate.

Similar limbs, clothing or silhouettes can suggest a comparison, but resemblance is not enough to label a remake model as a particular original enemy. This guide does not turn unsourced frame labels into a confirmed roster.
